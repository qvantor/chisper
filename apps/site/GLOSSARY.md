# Terms of the site in en, es and de

The words the texts of the site (`src/content`, `src/i18n`) use for one thing, in each language. A Spanish or German
text uses these and no others. A term that is not here is added here first, together with the variants it rules out.
The names of the synth (knobs, sections, modes, buttons, presets, tables) stay in English, as the synth shows them.

## Style

|                          | es                                                      | de                                                      |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| The reader               | tú                                                      | du; imperatives of du: Dreh, Zieh, Lade, Spiel          |
| A control of the synth   | its English name in bold: **Wave**, **Decay**, **Free** | its English name in bold: **Wave**, **Decay**, **Free** |
| A preset or a table      | its English name in italics: _Init_, _Basic Shapes_     | its English name in italics: _Init_, _Basic Shapes_     |
| Numbers and units        | 0,5 s · 25 % · 440 Hz (decimal comma, a space before)   | 0,5 s · 25 % · 440 Hz (decimal comma, a space before)   |
| A list of decimals       | 25; 12,5; 6,25 y 3,125 %                                | 25; 12,5; 6,25 und 3,125 %                              |
| The sections of the site | Manual, Aprende                                         | Anleitung, Lernen                                       |
